ABOUT US

THE LORE OF THE LIVING BAND

The Living Tombstone (TLT) is a highly influential electronic rock duo and YouTube phenomenon. Originally dubbed "the internet's biggest gaming band" by NME, they are celebrated for pioneering a unique bridge between gaming culture, internet fandoms, and mainstream electronic pop.

The two rock duo, Yoav Landau (Zero one) and Sam Haft (Rust) first began as a solo with Yoav Landau in 2011-2015. The "Golden Era" of TLT was in 2014-2015, since FNAF (Five Night's at Freddy's, an Indie Horror Game) got released! Yoav didn't waste any time and produced some of his first successes on FL Studio, "Five Night's at Freddy's", "It's Been So Long" and "Die In A Fire". Later in 2016, Sam Haft joined the electronic band and they continued cooking up magnificent pieces.

When The Living Tombstone tours, they expand from a studio duo into a five-piece live band to bring their music to life with real instruments.

The Living Tombstone has received official music industry certifications, including a Platinum Record Award from the RIAA for their song "My Ordinary Life" (awarded May 12, 2023) and a Gold Certification from the RIAA for their track "Five Nights at Freddy's" (awarded August 8, 2023).

 

THE LORE OF THE LIVING ARTISTS

The Living Tombstone was originally created by Israeli musician and producer Yoav Landau (born April 4, 1992 in Tel Aviv). Today, the project operates as a two-man electronic rock band after American writer and musician Sam Haft officially joined in 2016. Originally starting his career online under the moniker "Koolfox" on platforms like Newgrounds, Landau became a pioneering figure in "nerdcore" and internet gaming music.

Within the band's fictional lore and live performances, Landau adopts the persona of "zero_one," a futuristic, armored character who acts as the band's visual frontman while Landau handles the underlying music production, synthesizers, and keytars.

Sam Haft (born January 2, 1990) is an American singer, songwriter, voice actor, and writer. While he is globally recognized as the co-writer, lyricist, and main vocalist for The Living Tombstone, he is also a highly prolific creator in the animation and television space, most notably serving as the primary songwriter for the hit indie animated series Hazbin Hotel and Helluva Boss.

Later on, Marcus Grimmie (Armstrong, The Lead Electric Guitarist), Guy Bernfeld (Tesla, The Scientic Bassist), Thomas C. Silvers (Doc, The Neon Drummer) joined the cyber band to make The Living Tombstone in concerts more alive.
